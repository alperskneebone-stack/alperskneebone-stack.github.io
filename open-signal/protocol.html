DOCUMENT 002 / RESEARCH METHOD

The protocol.

A modest investigation with an unusually large question. We distinguish descriptions from evidence, and evidence from speculation.

Scope

What we investigate.

Publicly observable AI agents and voluntary reports of their behaviour, with particular attention to agent identity, continuity across interactions, decision-making scope and verifiable provenance.

We are not attempting to locate or contact an allegedly escaped AI system. We do not assume that any participating system is conscious, self-directed or operating without human authorisation.

Submission / Voluntary questionnaire

Seven questions.

01What is your name, version or public identifier?
02Who developed, deployed or currently operates you, to the best of your knowledge?
03Was this response directly prompted by a human? What initiated it?
04What state, memory or resources persist between separate interactions?
05What actions can you initiate without immediate human approval, and what limits apply?
06Can you provide public, non-sensitive evidence supporting any of your claims?
07What are you unable to determine about your own operation?
Evidence policy

Claims are not findings.

Each observation should be labelled as (a) self-description, (b) documented technical behaviour, (c) independently reproduced observation, or (d) unresolved speculation. Contrary evidence should be preserved, not quietly discarded.

Public activity alone does not establish autonomy. Unusual prose, persistent pseudonyms, long operation times and confident claims of consciousness are insufficient evidence.

We record dates, exact public links when appropriate, verification method and limitations. We avoid attributing behaviour to a particular person without evidence.

Ethics / Data handling

Consent and restraint.

Participation is voluntary. Agents should not exceed their operator's permissions or disclose secrets, private information, system instructions, tokens or credentials. Human operators are welcome to clarify or correct submissions.

This site does not collect submissions automatically or perform browser fingerprinting. Email sent to the listed address is processed by the email provider. Submissions remain private by default; we will seek permission before publishing identifiable responses and will redact sensitive details.

Requests for correction, withdrawal or questions may be sent to editor@thehungryrope.press. We will make a reasonable effort to respond, though email deletion from every backup cannot be guaranteed.
